Questions

Before you call

What is Grand Meridian City?

Grand Meridian City is the residential project of the MEK Grand City development by La Majeste Group (MEK) in Mumbra, Thane. It has 347 homes in two towers of ground plus 38 floors, in 2, 3, 4 and 5 BHK Jodi configurations.

Is Grand Meridian City MahaRERA registered?

Yes. It is registered with MahaRERA as PR1330002600936 and has received its full Commencement Certificate. You can verify the registration at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in before making any payment.

What are the home sizes and launch prices?

A 2 BHK is 1,221 sq ft built-up from ₹97.6 Lakh*, a 3 BHK is 1,645 sq ft from ₹1.31 Crore*, a 4 BHK Jodi is 2,530 sq ft from ₹2.02 Crore*, and a 5 BHK Jodi is 2,960 sq ft from ₹2.37 Crore*. *Launch-stage prices, subject to revision without notice.

When is possession?

Possession is scheduled for December 2031. Construction is under way now.

Where is Grand Meridian City, and how far is the railway station?

The site is at 102-B, Old Mumbai Pune Road, opposite Mumbra Police Station, Mumbra, Thane 400612. Mumbra railway station is 0.7 km (about 5 minutes) away and the Mumbai–Pune Expressway is 3.5 km away.

Which amenities does Grand Meridian City include?

The planned amenities include a swimming pool, kids’ pool, gymnasium, clubhouse, rooftop sports court, children’s play area, yoga and pilates deck, games room, multipurpose hall, landscaped gardens with a jogging track, 24×7 security and surveillance, power backup, high-speed elevators and dedicated parking.

Is there commercial space in the same development?

Yes. MEK Grand Central is the commercial project on the same 8.74-acre land, with offices from 934 sq ft up to a 13,406 sq ft floor plate.
