MEK Grand City's payment plan, explained milestone by milestone

A construction-linked payment plan ties what you pay to how far along the building actually is — you're not handing over the full amount upfront, and the developer isn't funding construction entirely on advance bookings either. Here's how MEK Grand Central's 16-stage schedule breaks down.

The full schedule

StageMilestoneCumulative %
1Earnest money10%
2Within 30 days of agreement execution30%
3Completion of the plinth35%
4Completion of podium levels40%
5–10Completion of 5th through terrace floor slabs45% → 70%
11Brickwork, door & window frames75%
12Internal & external plaster80%
13Plumbing & electrical fittings85%
14Flooring / tile work90%
15Painting work95%
16Possession100%

Why a construction-linked plan generally favours the buyer

Compared to a flat "10:90" or "20:80" scheme where most of the money is due far ahead of completion, a construction-linked plan spreads payments across each visible stage of progress. If construction stalls, your remaining payments stall with it — you're never paying meaningfully ahead of what's actually been built.

Halal, interest-free payment options

For buyers who want a financing structure without interest, MEK Grand City offers Halal, interest-free payment plans on request — this sits alongside the standard construction-linked schedule above rather than replacing it, so it's worth asking the sales desk for the specific structure that applies to your unit.

What to confirm before you sign

  • Whether the percentages are calculated on the full agreement value or a specific base price — confirm what GST, stamp duty, and registration are charged against separately.
  • What happens if a construction milestone is delayed — MahaRERA's delay-penalty provisions apply regardless of the payment plan's own structure.
  • Whether early or lump-sum payment ahead of a milestone is permitted, and if it comes with any discount.
See the full payment plan table and the Halal, interest-free option on the main site, or call/WhatsApp the sales desk directly.
