2 BHK vs 3 BHK vs 4 BHK Jodi: choosing the right configuration

Grand Meridian City offers four configurations — 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 4 BHK Jodi, and 5 BHK Jodi — and the honest way to choose between them isn't "which is the best deal per square foot." It's "which one matches the shape of your household, now and for the next several years." Here's each configuration mapped to the buyer it actually tends to suit.

2 BHK · 1,221 sq ft built-up · ₹97.6 Lakh*

Who it suits: a young couple, or a small family with one child, buying their first home. At 1,221 sq ft built-up, a 2 BHK gives you a proper separate bedroom for a child or for guests without paying for space you won't use day to day. It's also the configuration where the loan math is most forgiving — a lower principal means a lower EMI relative to most salaried incomes in this bracket, and it qualifies more easily against standard loan-to-income ratios that banks apply.

The trade-off is flexibility. A 2 BHK works well for the stage of life it's built for, but it doesn't leave much runway if your household grows faster than expected — a second child, a parent moving in, or a work-from-home setup that needs its own room.

3 BHK · 1,645 sq ft built-up · ₹1.31 Cr*

Who it suits: a growing family — typically a couple with two children, or a couple that wants a dedicated home office or study alongside the main bedrooms. The jump from 1,221 to 1,645 sq ft isn't just "one more room," it's enough additional area to give each child their own space as they grow older and need more privacy, while still keeping the flat manageable for a nuclear family to maintain.

This is usually the configuration buyers choose when they're not just solving for today, but deliberately buying ahead of a life stage they can see coming — a second child on the way, or children old enough that sharing a room is no longer practical.

4 BHK Jodi · 2,530 sq ft built-up · ₹2.02 Cr*

Who it suits: a joint or multi-generational household — parents living with adult children, or two generations sharing one home while still wanting genuine separation, not just extra bedrooms. A Jodi configuration joins two adjacent flats into one connected unit, which means you get the privacy benefits of separate living areas (sometimes separate entrances or kitchens, depending on the specific layout) combined under one address.

This matters more than it sounds for multi-generational families: it's the difference between "everyone shares one living room and one kitchen" and "each generation has a wing of their own, with the whole family still one doorbell away." For families where that boundary genuinely reduces friction, a Jodi configuration earns its premium.

5 BHK Jodi · 2,960 sq ft built-up · ₹2.37 Cr*

Who it suits: a large family that needs the Jodi structure but has outgrown even the 4 BHK version — three generations under one roof, several children, or a household that regularly hosts extended family for long stretches. At 2,960 sq ft, this is less a "home" in the single-family sense and more a private compound that happens to share one address, which is exactly the point for families at this scale.

The right configuration isn't the one that fits your household today — it's the one that still fits comfortably three to five years from now, without forcing you to move again before the loan is even halfway paid off.

One thing worth checking regardless of configuration

Whichever size you choose, confirm whether the price you've been quoted is based on built-up area or a different area measure — the figures above are all built-up area, and comparing a built-up-area quote against a carpet-area quote from another project will make the two look more different in value than they actually are.
