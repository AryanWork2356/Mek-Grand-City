Inside MEK Grand City's amenities: what 8.74 acres actually gets you

Amenity lists in brochures tend to blur together — every project claims a "clubhouse" and a "landscaped garden." What actually matters is scale and what's genuinely built into the land, not just promised on paper. At MEK Grand City, the residential and commercial towers share one 8.74-acre gated parcel, with amenities split across both the home side (Grand Meridian City) and the office side (MEK Grand Central).

On the residential side: Grand Meridian City

Two towers of ground plus 38 floors sit inside a landscaped, walled enclave rather than a standalone high-rise. The residential amenity deck includes a gymnasium, clubhouse, landscaped gardens with a jogging track, a dedicated cycling track, an outdoor fitness zone, a rooftop sports court, and a children's play area. For families, there's also a senior citizen area, a party and family lawn, and a dedicated kids' pool alongside the main swimming pool.

Day-to-day essentials are covered too: 24×7 security and surveillance, power backup, fire safety systems, dedicated and valet parking, high-speed elevators, concierge services, and an elegant drop-off court at the entrance. Indoors, a multipurpose hall, a visitor lounge, a community and social lounge, a reading and lounge area, and a games room round out the amenity set.

On the commercial side: MEK Grand Central

The office tower is purpose-built commercial space, not converted residential stock, and its amenities reflect that: high-speed elevators, a grand entrance lobby, multi-tier security, 24×7 power backup, advanced fire-fighting systems, ample vehicle parking, Vastu-compliant layouts, and high-speed Wi-Fi zoning throughout the building.

For businesses that need to host clients or run meetings, there are dedicated conference zones and executive lounge areas, plus wide column-free layouts and a high-visibility glass facade that doubles as signage for ground-floor retail and office tenants.

Why "shared 8.74 acres" matters more than it sounds

A lot of projects in this price bracket sit on a single compact plot with amenities squeezed around the building's footprint. Here, both towers draw from one larger landscaped parcel — which is part of why the amenity list runs as long as it does on both sides, residential and commercial, without either one feeling like an afterthought.
