01

Overview

Commercial from the foundation up, not converted from flats

Along much of Old Mumbai Pune Road, offices sit in flats that were drawn as homes. MEK Grand Central starts from a different brief: column-free floor plates, a glass frontage that works as signage, and a clean MahaRERA registration, PC1330002600951.

  • A

    Planned as workplaces

    Floors are laid out for offices and shops from the first drawing, rather than adapted from residential plans.

  • B

    Space that scales

    Column-free plates run from a 934 sq ft boutique unit to a 13,406 sq ft full floor, so the layout can follow the business.

  • C

    A face to the road

    A high-visibility glass facade and a double-height arrival lobby give tenants and visitors a clear point of arrival.

The same 8.74-acre land also holds Grand Meridian City, the development’s 347 homes, so an owner can keep a workplace here and a home there without leaving the address. For the full development, discover MEK Grand City.

Questions

Answers before you call

What is MEK Grand Central?

MEK Grand Central is the commercial project of the MEK Grand City development by La Majeste Group (MEK) in Mumbra, Thane, offering offices and retail space on Old Mumbai Pune Road.

Is MEK Grand Central MahaRERA registered?

Yes. It is registered with MahaRERA as PC1330002600951 and has received its full Commencement Certificate. The registration can be verified at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in before any payment is made.

What office sizes and prices are available?

A boutique office is 934 sq ft built-up from ₹1.87 Crore*, an executive suite is 1,528 sq ft from ₹3.06 Crore*, a premium office is 2,128 sq ft from ₹4.26 Crore*, and a corporate office is 2,902 sq ft from ₹5.80 Crore*. A full floor plate of 13,406 sq ft is available on request. *Launch-stage prices, subject to revision without notice.

Is there office space for sale near Mumbra railway station?

Yes. MEK Grand Central is about 0.7 km from Mumbra railway station on Old Mumbai Pune Road, with offices from a 934 sq ft boutique unit up to a 13,406 sq ft full floor plate.

When is possession?

Possession is scheduled for December 2031. Construction is under way now.

What building infrastructure does MEK Grand Central have?

Planned features include high-speed elevators, a grand entrance lobby, multi-tier security, 24×7 power backup, advanced fire-fighting systems, ample vehicle parking, high-speed Wi-Fi zoning, dedicated conference zones, executive lounge areas, wide column-free layouts and a high-visibility glass facade.

Can I also buy a home in the same development?

Yes. Grand Meridian City is the residential project on the same 8.74-acre land, with 2, 3, 4 and 5 BHK Jodi homes in two towers of ground plus 38 floors.
