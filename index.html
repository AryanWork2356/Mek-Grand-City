Why here

The infrastructure argument stopped being a promise

Mumbra was priced as a fringe address while its connectivity was still on paper. Most of it has since been built. The pricing has not fully caught up, and that gap is the whole case.

  1. Open since January 2024

    Atal Setu

    India's longest sea bridge cut the run between Navi Mumbai and south Mumbai to about twenty minutes, pulling this whole eastern belt closer to the island city.

  2. Flying since December 2025

    Navi Mumbai airport

    Domestic operations began in December 2025, with international flights scheduled from July 2026. A second aviation hub on this side of the harbour.

  3. Phase 5A due December 2026

    Metro Line 5

    The Thane to Bhiwandi to Kalyan line takes daily pressure off the Central railway and reprices everything along its corridor.
