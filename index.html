The infrastructure argument stopped being a promise

Mumbra was priced as a fringe address while its connectivity was still on paper. Most of it has since been built. The pricing has not fully caught up, and that gap is the whole case.

  1. Open since January 2024

    Atal Setu

    India's longest sea bridge cut the run between Navi Mumbai and south Mumbai to about twenty minutes, pulling this whole eastern belt closer to the island city.

  2. Flying since December 2025

    Navi Mumbai airport

    Domestic operations began in December 2025, with international flights scheduled from July 2026. A second aviation hub on this side of the harbour.

  3. Phase 5A due December 2026

    Metro Line 5

    The Thane to Bhiwandi to Kalyan line takes daily pressure off the Central railway and reprices everything along its corridor.

Questions buyers ask first

Who is the developer?

Both projects are built by LA MAJESTE GROUP (MEK). Grand Meridian City is the residential project on Old Mumbai Pune Road, Mumbra. MEK Grand Central is the commercial project on the same road.

Are the projects MahaRERA registered?

Yes. Grand Meridian City is PR1330002600936 and MEK Grand Central is PC1330002600951. Both can be checked on the MahaRERA portal before you book.

When do I get possession?

December 2031 for both, with launch in June 2026. Both are under construction now.

What sizes are available?

Homes are 2 BHK at 749 sq ft carpet and 3 BHK at 997 sq ft carpet, across two towers of 347 units. Offices run from a 467 sq ft boutique unit to a 5,453 sq ft full floor plate.

What do prices start at?

₹99 Lakhs for a 2 BHK, ₹1.31 Crore for a 3 BHK, and ₹77.06 Lakhs for the smallest office. These are launch-stage prices and they revise as construction progresses.

Can I get a home loan on this?

Yes. MahaRERA-registered projects are eligible for standard home and commercial loan products. Tell us your requirement and we will walk you through the payment schedule.

Can I visit the site?

Yes, seven days a week. Pickup can be arranged from Mumbra or Thane station. Call 98217 02210 to book a slot.

Launch pricing does not survive the launch

Both projects are pre-launch through June 2026. First-allocation buyers take the best inventory, the lowest entry and the longest payment runway. Tell us what you are after and we will send the price sheet, the payment plan and current availability.
